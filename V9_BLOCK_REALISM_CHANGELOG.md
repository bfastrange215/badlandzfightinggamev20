# Philly Badlandz V9 — Block Realism & Idle Personality Pass

## Stage realism
- Rebuilt North Philly Block rowhomes at a larger, more believable scale.
- Added deeper house facades, stoops, window rows, and fire-escape detail.
- Improved curb/sidewalk reads and street surface accents.

## Character look pass
- Tweaked body proportions to look less toy-like, especially on female fighters.
- Improved Lexa's longer hair silhouette while keeping Lucy/Chucky shorter.

## Stance personality
- Added per-fighter idle motion profiles so each stance has its own posture, bounce, and guard rhythm.
- Stronger read between pressure boxer, kickboxer, brawler, trickster, grappler, enforcer, wild scrapper, counter striker, and aerial specialist.

## Notes
- Multiplayer, rooms, abilities, combos, rounds, and UI are preserved from V8.
- Procedural fighters remain in use; this is a realism/polish pass, not a full photoreal character asset replacement.
